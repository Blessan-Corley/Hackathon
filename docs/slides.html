Slide 5: Trade-offs, AI Usage, Limitations & Roadmap

05 / 05

The Rejected Decision

Rejected: Automated AI Deal Value Sizing ($ ARR) & Win Probability Scoring.

Why: Zero CRM deal tables or historical contracts exist. Guessing numbers violates data integrity and creates false managerial confidence.

Trade-off Accepted: Reporting qualitative stage blockers, named owners, and competitor identification (StoreHarbor C05).

AI Usage & Determinism

  • 100% Deterministic Engine: Replay ordering, deduplication, revision replacement, and metrics run deterministically.
  • Pre-generated Dossiers: All 18 transcripts are analyzed with turn citations and embedded locally, eliminating API key requirements.

Limitations & Future Work

Local speaker labels (Team member A, Customer A) are scoped per meeting; future enhancements will link attendees across meetings via corporate email domains.
